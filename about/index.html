About the artist

Pi3rre-Luc Therrien

Artist, author, designer, filmmaker — and the founder of Syntheris Studios in Montreal.

Art has been my lifelong passion, starting with a contest win at age 4 and evolving into a 25-year journey building an original comic book universe. As the founder of Syntheris Studios, I now blend traditional artistry with AI to craft comics, graphic novels, short films, and collectibles that challenge and inspire.

My diverse experiences — from military service to curating immersive art events — have honed my skills in leadership and creative direction. Through Syntheris Studios, I aim to create unique, high-quality content that captivates audiences and redefines the intersection of art and technology.

Syntheris Studios is a production business and publishing company. The work is the point: original stories, published as comics and graphic novels, developed as animated feature films, collected as designer toys — and the production software built in-house to make it all.

— Pi3rre-Luc
Lead Creative and Founder of Syntheris Studios
The studio

Syntheris Studios Inc.

  • Based in Montreal, Canada. The studio works from Montreal, publishing and producing for audiences everywhere.
  • Production first. Comics, graphic novels, animated films, designer toys — original IP, made in-house from first sketch to finished product.
  • Publishing in-house. The studio's own imprint for original comic books and graphic novels, launching a new comic book universe with its first book on Kickstarter.
  • Software, built in-house. The production tools the studio builds for its own work — including Syntheris Continuum, the frame interpolation tool, released open source under the MIT licence.
  • Enquiries welcome. Premium creative services and Generative AI consulting are available to a small number of clients, by enquiry only.
