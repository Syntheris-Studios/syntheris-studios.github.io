Privacy Policy

This site collects almost nothing. Here is exactly what happens with the little it does.

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Syntheris Studios Inc. ("the studio") operates syntheris-studios.com. This policy explains what information the site collects and what the studio does with it.

What this site collects

Almost nothing. This is a static website: its pages are plain files served to your browser. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, no cookies set by the studio, no analytics, and no advertising trackers.

Email enquiries

If you write to info@syntheris-studios.com, the studio receives your email address and the contents of your message. That information is used only to read and reply to your enquiry. It is never sold or shared with third parties.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. GitHub and Google may log requests to their own servers under their own privacy policies. Links to the studio's social pages (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X, GitHub) take you to those platforms, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. This policy will be reviewed by legal counsel at incorporation.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@syntheris-studios.com.
